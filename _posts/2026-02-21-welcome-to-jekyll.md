This site is where I’ve been putting together everything from my Reading Like A Computer class at NYU Abu Dhabi. Instead of thinking of it as a portfolio, I see it more as a record of how my ideas changed over time, what I tried, what worked, and what didn’t.

The class itself pushes you to think about reading in a way that feels a bit unnatural at first. Not just reading for meaning, but thinking about how a computer would process the same text, what it notices, what it ignores, and how tools and algorithms can reshape what we think a text is even saying.

Because of that, the work here isn’t just one thing. Some parts are more analytical, some are more creative, and some are just me experimenting and figuring things out as I go. It’s a mix of writing, technical work, and small interactive pieces that all connect back to that main idea of how meaning gets made.
